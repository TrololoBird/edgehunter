# Archive research findings (moved from CLAUDE.md)

## Audit eng-7 research
- Channels with the stop on the structure's own timeframe (`research_channels.py`, `research/channels.md`: 4h / 1d regression channels whose envelope includes every wick, entry at the boundary after a confirming candle of the same timeframe, stop beyond the envelope + 0.5 ATR, targets the middle / the opposite side): the boundary breaks MORE often than geometry says (TP1 hit below the random-walk rate in most cells), no cell positive in both halves.
- Pumps by size (`research/pumps_by_size.md`): fading pumps of 60%+ is the worst (TP1 40% vs 63% geometry, -0.19R); buying their first pullback 71% vs 48% (+0.24R, 75 trades: a hypothesis, too few to act on).
- **Card replay on the archive** (`research_cards.py`, `research/cards_6m.md`): SWEEP / BREAKOUT events by the live detectors, planned by the live strategies, walked through the shared lifecycle minute by minute (zone, 5m structure-shift trigger, `low_rr` / breakout `no_oi` vetoes, INVALID / MISSED / EXPIRED) and replayed like the journal: 202k cards on 360 coins over 6 months, ~2% reach an entry (most triggers are `low_rr`: after the shift the stop beyond the wick is far - the live journal shows the same: every sweep entry vetoed) and every class loses net of costs (breakout up -0.18R, down -0.13R, sweep low -0.27R, sweep high -0.06R n.s.); the vetoed triggers would have lost too (-0.13..-0.18R), sweeps against the 1h trend are worse than with it. **`sweep` and `breakout` cards are therefore disabled by default** (`CONFIG.strategies.enabled`; still detected and journaled with `vetoed='disabled'`). The phase setups replayed the same way (`--what phases`, `research/phases_6m.md`: live scorer, perp-led / tide vetoes, the plan re-made at each 15m close, the spring test): BEAR C -0.24R, BULL A -0.20R, BULL F -0.25R, BULL B -0.14R, and momentum (Tier-0 impulses probed like pretraining) IMPULSE UP -0.29R, BEAR D -0.19R (q <= 0.09) -> their cards are off too (`CONFIG.strategies.disabled_setups`); BULL E (+0.32R, 45 entries, n.s.; the 12-month replay after the MTF fix below: -0.17R on 171 entries, so its plus was noise — disabled in eng-9 by the owner's decision, still journaled) and IMPULSE DOWN (+0.06R, n.s.) stay on, with absorption and exhaustion (not replayable on the archive). Holdout (`--end 2026-03-30`, the half-year before, not used for the decisions; `research/cards_holdout.md`): every disabled setup negative again (-0.11..-0.27R), BULL E +0.10R and IMPULSE DOWN +0.14R again positive but not significant. Tests run with every strategy enabled (`conftest.all_strategies_enabled`).
- **12-month attempts to make the cards pay** (401 coins, dev = older half, one holdout check on the newer half): 12 fixed exit rules (`research_exits.py`, `research/exits_12m.md`: breakeven after +0.5% / +1R, trailing, time exits, 1/2/3R targets) - none positive on the holdout (best: 60-min time exit with a limit entry, -0.07% a trade); a meta-model (`research_meta.py`, `research/meta_12m.md`, LightGBM AUC 0.63) selects trades at -0.10R vs -0.16R for all - not shipped; a limit (maker) entry (`RESEARCH_ENTRY=maker`, `research/maker_12m.md`) fills fewer trades and averages -0.205R (adverse selection outweighs the fee saving); daily TSMOM / XSMOM / EMA trend (`research_trend_daily.py`, `research/trend_daily.md`) do not replicate on 2023-10..2026-09. Gross R of the card trades is ~0: the loss is the cost, the signals carry no edge to pay it.
- **Higher timeframes by market phase** (`research_htf.py`, `research/htf_phases.md`: 1h archive, 478 coins, 2023-10..2026-08, phase = BTC vs its 200-day SMA + breadth above the 50-day SMA, known at the previous close; dev older half, one holdout check): Elder's triple screen (weekly / daily tide, 4h pullback, 1h entry, 3-ATR chandelier, up to 14 days), Turtle 20/10 daily and Bollinger range reversion - no strategy x direction x phase cell passes (q <= 0.10); triple-screen longs in the UP phase -0.12R dev / -0.40R holdout. Wide stops (4-20%) shrink the cost to ~0.03R, but the gross result is still ~0. The published ensemble Donchian (Zarattini et al. 2025, `research_ensemble.py`, `research/ensemble_trend.md`): +3.1% a year on 2021-02..2025-03 (the paper: 18% on 2015-2025), -1.6% a year after publication; the phase gate alone (top 20 held only in UP) +20% a year to 2025-03 with a -48% drawdown, -3.9% after.
- **Attempts to make the entries profitable, all judged on the newer half after choosing on the older one** (12 months, 401 coins, 332k replayed trades with entry features and twelve exit rules on the same path: `research_cards.py --samples`): exit rules (`research_exits.py`: breakeven, trailing, time, fixed targets, wider stops) - none positive on the holdout (best: a 60-min exit with a limit entry, -0.07% a trade); meta-labeling (`research_meta.py`, LightGBM / logistic, TP1-before-stop and net-R targets) - AUC 0.63 but the selected trades still lose (-0.10R); literature replacements on daily candles (`research_trend_daily.py`: TSMOM, XSMOM, EMA trend, 36 months) - none positive vs the universe mean. Even at zero cost the cards' trades average ~+0.01R (TP1 +2.34% on 26% of trades, stops -1.22% on 58%). `research_meta.py` never takes the `x_*` exit-outcome columns as features (they leaked the label once: AUC 1.000).
- **MTF geometry fix** (2026-09-28): a trade on a 1h / 4h leg lived 8h like a 15m one and its targets were spaced in 15m ATRs; now the trade TTL scales with the leg (x2 / x4) and targets use the leg's own ATR. Replayed on the same 12 months (`research/cards_fixed_12m.md` vs `research/cards_12m.md`): more reversal entries pass (BEAR C 12.6k vs 8.3k), results barely move (BEAR C -0.18R vs -0.19R, BULL A -0.22R vs -0.24R), BULL E flips to -0.17R (171 entries; +0.20R before: its sign was noise), IMPULSE DOWN +0.07R. Exit geometry cannot create an edge the entries do not have: per-coin / per-volatility tuning of stop and target walk-forward (`research_adaptive.py`, `research/adaptive_params.md`) is worse than one geometry for all coins (-0.049R vs -0.036R), and even a look-ahead monthly choice reaches only +0.029R; ATR and fixed-% stops behave alike (the loss is the cost share of the stop).
- **Positioning as the entry source** (`research_positioning.py`, `research/positioning.md`: funding level / z, global and top-trader long/short z, 3-day OI and price change, 349 coins, 2025-10..2026-08, forward 24h / 72h vs the universe mean by decile): only the funding LEVEL passed the in-sample rule (the most negative decile kept underperforming, -0.47% / -1.91% over 72h); its pre-registered holdout (`research_funding.py`, `research/funding_holdout.md`: short when the last settlement <= -0.0317%, 72h, 2024-01..2025-09) is NOT confirmed: -1.10% a trade net, -0.43% vs the universe mean, the short pays +0.59% funding over the hold.
- **Rejections at equal highs / lows** (`research_eqh.py`, `research/eqh.md`, 478 coins, 2023-10..2026-08): a 1h rejection at the most-touched intact EQH / EQL of the last 168h, with / against the daily trend, 2-3 / 4-6 / 7+ touches, stops 0.5 or 2 ATR beyond, targets 1x / 2x: the hit rate is at the random-walk rate or below in every cell (longs 43-48% vs 50%, shorts 46-53%); touches and the daily trend change nothing.

#### Scenario card (`scenarios.py`, `macro.py`, `train_scenarios.py`)
- Every coin panel shows a two-sided scenario card on the 1h map (the format of popular level posts): the reaction zone the price is in, up to 3 resistances above and 2 supports below (1h / 4h swings, equal highs / lows, previous day / week / sessions, the 8-day 1h volume profile, round numbers; zones of strength >= `min_strength`, the first >= 0.5 ATR(1h) beyond the reaction zone, then >= 1 ATR apart), a LONG plan (1h close above the reaction zone + retest -> the resistances, stop below the first support) and a SHORT plan (a rejection at the first resistance -> the supports), R:R net of costs, plus the FRED macro context (US10Y, broad dollar, Brent; 5-day changes, known from the day after). Information, never an order.
- The probability "first resistance before first support within 72h" (`train_scenarios.py`: 478 coins, 2023-10..2026-08, every 12h, 396k samples, time split train / isotonic calibration / test): the driftless random-walk geometry d_down / (d_up + d_down) is almost perfectly calibrated (bins 0.35 -> 33.5%, 0.55 -> 53.6%, 0.65 -> 64%), level strength adds nothing (at p ~ 0.5: 48.2% vs 48.7% whichever side is stronger), and LightGBM on price / BTC / trend features (+ macro, rejected on the calibration part) is WORSE out of sample (Brier 0.2318 vs 0.2207) - it leans on BTC's last week, a regime that changed. So the card shows the geometric probability, labelled as such (`models/scenario_prob.pkl` is used only when saved with passed=True). Trading the call from the price (p >= 0.6 long / <= 0.4 short, target / stop = the two levels) loses -0.06R net: the levels are where price goes as often as a random walk says, not more (`research/scenario_model.md`).

- The card also carries the higher-timeframe range (`scenarios.levels_htf`: intact daily swings of 90 days, previous week / month high-low, the 30-day high / low, the 30-day volume profile POC / VAH / VAL / HVN; weights `swing_1d` / `htf` 2.5; inputs `MarketData.htf_1d` / `htf_1h_30d` loaded by `load_htf`) and `htf_context` (weekly EMA20 trend, place in the 30-day range). Its four triggers were replayed on the archive (`research_level_triggers.py`, `research/level_triggers.md`: 478 coins, 2024-01..2026-08, 772k trades): breakout-retest long, breakdown-retest short, rejection short at the first resistance, bounce long at the first support - each compared with the random-walk hit rate of the SAME entry / stop / target. None beats it by more than ~2 pp in both halves: longs -2..-3 pp, shorts +0.5..+2 pp (the alt market's downward drift of 2024-26, not the trigger); with / against the weekly trend and the place in the 30-day range change nothing; every trigger is net negative to TP1 (-0.02..-0.15R). The card shows these measured rates next to each plan (`TRIGGER_STATS`).

#### Small-cap pumps / dumps (`pumps.py`, `research_pumps.py`)
- Coin classes behave differently, so they are measured apart: majors (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB) vs small caps (outside the 30 most traded that day). On majors the level triggers above do not beat geometry either (-0.10..-0.13R, weekly trend changes nothing).
- A PUMP = close >= 15% above the 6h low with the move's hours >= 3x the average hour of the week before (DUMP mirrored). After it (`research/pumps.md`, 2023-10..2026-08, ~4k small-cap events): 73-82% of pumps come back to 50% of the move before a new high, 30-39% to its base; fading the pump (short on the first close below the previous low) is not better than geometry. Pre-registered holdout 2021-04..2023-09 (`research/pumps_holdout.md`): **short the first 38.2-61.8% bounce after a DUMP** (1h close below the previous low, stop beyond the bounce, TP1 the dump's low) held in all four periods (+0.16..+0.36R, TP1 62-69% vs 45-57% geometry; small samples, 86-403 trades each); buying the first pullback after a PUMP did NOT (hit rate at the geometry in 2021-23).
- `pumps.monitor` shows the live small-cap pumps / dumps of the last 48h on the Forming tab (`build_state['pumps']`): the move, base, peak, the pullback zone, its state and the trigger's levels, with each rule's measured verdict. Information, never an order.
